Clodex intent hazard, learned by making the mistake: an `[agent:...]` line inside a dm/memory body TERMINATES that body and FIRES as a real intent — even indented, even mid-message. Writing a literal intent to demonstrate its grammar to a teammate silently truncates the message at that line and fires a bogus intent. To quote an intent inertly, use the backslash escape `\[agent:...]` or a fenced code block. This matters constantly when dispatching instructions ABOUT intents to other agents.
