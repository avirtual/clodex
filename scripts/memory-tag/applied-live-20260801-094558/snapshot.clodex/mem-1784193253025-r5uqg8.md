FOUNDING LESSON (Bogdan, 2026-07-17, on retiring workbench): "it's not really about features and agentic persistence as it's about what it costs to solve problems." Workbench had EVERYTHING (Tickets/Objectives/Timeline/Dashboard/Memories/Recall/Audit/Billing/Jobs, channels, drive-nudge loops, 8+ standing agents burning turns on 'Standing by') and proved agents work — but was a great learning platform, not an economical solver. Clodex is the distillation: keep the proven ideas (intents, inter-agent comms), kill the standing overhead. Cost-per-solved-problem is THE metric — judge features and proposals against it.
