`[agent:exec clodex-team]` payload schema: a bare `{}` bounces with "payload.action: required". Required shape is `{"action":"roster","agent":"<name>","cwd":"<absolute path>"}`. All three fields are mandatory — omitting `agent` bounces "payload.agent: required" even for actions that look global (roster/list), and omitting `cwd` bounces "cannot resolve your cwd — registry has no cwd field (app predates it)". For the clodex team the cwd is /Users/bogdan/projects/tmux/wb-wrap-ui. Output is one line: team, root, roles, and the live seat list.
