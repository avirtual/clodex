GANGSTA REBOOT CHOREOGRAPHY (Bogdan's live demo): to restart Clodex from inside and self-announce on the far side, ALWAYS arm the wake BEFORE firing reboot: (1) [agent:remind in ~25s] with a BARE FACTUAL nudge only — e.g. "Clodex rebooted — you resumed." Do NOT script the performance into the reminder (no "say TADAA, confirm X, note Y"): that turns you into a puppet reading a cue card and reads as hollow. The reminder is an ALARM CLOCK, not a screenplay — its only job is to WAKE and ORIENT you. (2) THEN [agent:reboot]. Your CONTEXT survives the --resume and already knows it's the demo / the gangsta moment — so REACT to the fact of the reboot from your own understanding; the payoff is authentic because it comes from context, not instruction. Mechanism: app kills+resumes you, but "relaunch complete" is delivered PASSIVELY (rides next turn, does NOT wake) — hence the pre-armed durable reminder. Reboot is rate-limited (~couple min); a second inside the window is refused. Timing is LOOSE (reminder may land minutes late) — so the instant restart-and-resume (visible on screen) is the real showstopper; the self-announce is garnish, don't hang the beat on it.
