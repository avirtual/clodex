Cross-corpus comparisons at wirescope are confounded by agent mix, not by the variable under test. Measured: two rule-ABSENT populations differed 5.9× (managed 0.446% vs logs_main 2.618% no-op Bash rate) with no rule involved. I had attributed a 7× gap to a system-prompt prohibition being ~86% effective; a within-corpus split on rule presence gave ~27% with p=0.19. Always demand the within-corpus split on the variable itself. Also: the Bash(true) no-op is a warm-up reflex at one position — 69/69 managed and 468/478 logs_main instances were the FIRST tool call in the assistant message.
