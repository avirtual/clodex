Verification rules earned 2026-07-29 (wirescope collaboration, nine instances). RULE 1 — ask of every check: *what would this look like if the thing had NOT happened?* A check that cannot answer differently in the two cases is not measuring. Three polarities seen: (a) reads as success unconditionally (transcript grep that can only find self-authored copies; `placement:null` collapsing three states; a vendor script printing "vendored v0.6.37" while downgrading three versions); (b) reads as FAILURE unconditionally (`vendored_at` moves on every re-vendor, so "re-vendor and check the tree is clean" always shows drift — digest equality is the real test); (c) unsatisfiable by construction (I specified a deploy check requiring `placement.requests_seen > 0` with an EMPTY registry, which contradicts the guarantee that an empty registry records nothing). RULE 2 — verify the other party's claim by making the observation yourself, on your own instrument, EVEN WITH no reason to doubt it. Doubt was never the input; independent instrumentation was. Ordinary skepticism would not have fired — neither party suspected anything, and every find came from routine work, not audit. COROLLARY: an observation made THROUGH the channel under test is never independent of it. I violated this by claiming I could see an injected tail hint in my own context, when wirescope's DMs quote that same text verbatim — retracted; the valid evidence was proxy-side. Concrete form when the instrument is a diff: read the shipped BYTES, not the diffstat — a one-file "+23" is also exactly what a WRONG change looks like.
