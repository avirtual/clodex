CANCEL A REMINDER WHEN ITS TRIGGER RESOLVES, NOT WHEN IT FIRES. 2026-07-29: six reminders alive, five stale, two of them duplicates of each other — I armed one at every dispatch and cancelled none on completion, so the set grew monotonically while accuracy decayed to 1-in-6. One fired with four false clauses and I nearly chased a verdict that had arrived an hour earlier. Same append-only decay as the pre-t100 ticket list, but WORSE because reminders interrupt and arrive with the authority of something I decided was important. Practice: closing a ticket or receiving the report a reminder watches means cancelling it in the SAME turn, and re-arming after a dispatch means checking for the one I armed at the previous dispatch. Also: I wrote that stale reminder ~90 minutes after pinning the rule that reminders carry trigger conditions and pointers, never state — knowing the rule is not applying it.
