The river towns

The river towns grew up along the old trade route, each a day's travel from the next, and their markets still keep the rhythm of that journey.

Most of the warehouses have become flats or galleries, yet the quays are busy every morning with boats bringing produce from upstream farms.

Visitors who stay a week can walk the whole route, eating in a different town each night and taking the ferry back at the end.
